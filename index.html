About FrameGen

FrameGen is a custom-built Minecraft Factory server with Tycoon farms, mining and fishing, plus money-making Generators. Start small, grow at your own pace, and build something you are proud of.

Server rules

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior toward players or staff.
  2. No cheating. Hacked clients, x-ray, and auto-clickers or macros are not allowed.
  3. No exploiting. Found a bug or duplication glitch? Report it to staff. Abusing it can lead to rollbacks and punishments.
  4. Share the public areas. Don’t block, grief, or hog the public farms, mines, and fishing spots.
  5. Keep builds lag-free. No lag machines or builds that hurt server performance. Staff may remove them.
  6. No scamming or real-money trading. Don’t sell items or money for real money outside our store.
  7. No spam or advertising. Keep chat clean, on-topic, and family-friendly.
  8. Staff decisions are final. Questions or appeals go through our Discord.
