About FrameGen

FrameGen is a custom-built Minecraft Factory server with Tycoon farms, mining and fishing, plus money-making Generators. Start small, grow at your own pace, and build something you are proud of.

Server rules

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or toxic behavior.
  2. No cheating. Hacked clients and unfair advantages are not allowed.
  3. No exploiting. Found a bug? Report it to staff instead of abusing it.
  4. No spam or advertising. Keep chat clean and on-topic.
  5. Keep it family-friendly. No inappropriate language or builds.
  6. No griefing or scamming. Respect other players’ islands and trades.
  7. Staff decisions are final. Questions or appeals go through our Discord.
